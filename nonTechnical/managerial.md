# Qwist – Managerial Interview Questions

For the Qwist interview, I'd prioritize these questions:

1. Tell me about a project you owned end-to-end.

2. Tell me about a difficult technical decision you made.

3. Tell me about a production incident you handled.

4. How do you decide between an MVP and a production-grade solution?

5. How do you prioritize competing requirements?

6. Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate or manager.

7. How do you handle ambiguous requirements?

8. How do you ensure quality while moving quickly?

9. How do you use AI in your engineering workflow?

10. What would you do in your first 90 days at Qwist?
